Anyone who's been programming long enough will have had the experience of working really hard to solve a problem and getting stuck, then going to sleep and waking up the next morning with everything making perfect sense. I love when that happens. Sleep is OP

Today I fixed the issues I found in hot-reload. That feature's still not bullet proof, but it seems like any remaining issues aren't showstopping.

The rest of today I went back to working on making the UI customizable. I want to make it really easy to rearrange the interface so users have control over their workflow. Tools should adapt to users, not the other way around.
